What the graph shows

The voltage and current follow the same sine wave, so they rise and fall together. Because the resistor obeys V = IR and R = 50 Ω, a 2 A current produces a 100 V voltage. The moving marker shows the current point in time, and the plotted curves update smoothly to make the relationship easy to see.
