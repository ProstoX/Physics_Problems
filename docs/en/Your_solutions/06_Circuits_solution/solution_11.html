Problem Statement

Power Formula
P = V² / R
Power = Voltage squared divided by Resistance
1 Use the formula:
P = V² / R
2 Substitute the values:
P = 50² / 100
3 Calculate the numerator:
P = 2500 / 100
4 Final power:
P = 25 W
Energy Formula
E = P × t
Energy = Power multiplied by Time
1 Use the formula:
E = P × t
2 Substitute the values:
E = 25 × 300
3 Multiply:
E = 7500 J
