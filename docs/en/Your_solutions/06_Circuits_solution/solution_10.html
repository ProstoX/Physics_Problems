Average Current from a Lightning Strike

A lightning bolt transfers a large amount of charge in a very short time. Here, we calculate the average current using charge and time interval.

Formula: Iavg = ΔQ / Δt
A lightning bolt can carry a huge charge in just milliseconds, producing a very large current.

Step-by-step calculation

Final answer

15000 A

Explanation: Average current is charge transferred per unit time. Since 2 ms = 0.002 s, a charge of 30 C in 0.002 s gives a very large current of 15000 A. That is why lightning is such an intense electrical event.
