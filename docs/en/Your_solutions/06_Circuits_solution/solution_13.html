Transformer Ratio Visualization

Learn how transformer turns ratios determine voltage conversion.

Symbol Meanings

Vp = primary voltage (volts)
Vs = secondary voltage (volts)
Np = primary turns
Ns = secondary turns
Vs / Vp = Ns / Np

Given Values

Vp = 120 V
Vs = 9.0 V
Np = 400 turns

Step-by-Step Solution

1 Substitute the known values into the transformer equation.
9.0 / 120 = Ns / 400
2 Rearrange the equation to solve for Ns.
Ns = (9.0 × 400) / 120
3 Compute the result.
Ns = 30 turns
Final Answer
Secondary turns = 30
