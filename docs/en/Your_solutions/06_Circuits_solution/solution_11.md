## 11. Power & Energy

What is the power dissipated by a $100\,\Omega$ resistor when a voltage of 50 V is applied across it? How much energy is consumed in 5 minutes?

---

### Given

* **Resistance ($R$):** $100\,\Omega$
* **Voltage ($V$):** $50\text{ V}$
* **Time ($t$):** $5\text{ minutes}$

**Conversion:**
Convert time to seconds for standard SI units:


$$t = 5 \times 60 = 300\text{ s}$$

---

### Step 1: Calculate the Power

We use the power formula for a resistor:


$$P = \frac{V^2}{R}$$

Substitute the known values:


$$P = \frac{50^2}{100}$$

$$P = \frac{2500}{100}$$

$$P = 25\text{ W}$$

**Power Dissipated:**


$$\boxed{25\text{ W}}$$

---

### Step 2: Calculate the Energy Consumed

We use the energy formula:


$$E = P \times t$$

Substitute the values calculated in Step 1:


$$E = 25\text{ W} \times 300\text{ s}$$

$$E = 7,500\text{ J}$$

**Energy Consumed:**


$$\boxed{7,500\text{ J}}$$
