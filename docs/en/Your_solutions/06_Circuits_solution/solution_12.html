Transformer Voltage and Current

See how turns ratio and power balance determine the output voltage and current in an ideal transformer.

Problem: A transformer has a primary coil with 1000 turns and a secondary coil with 200 turns. If the primary voltage is 120 V (AC), what is the secondary voltage? If the current in the secondary is 3 A, what is the current in the primary?

Vs

Is

Transformer illustration

Equation 1: Voltage ratio

Vs / Vp = Ns / Np
Secondary voltage divided by primary voltage equals secondary turns divided by primary turns.
1
Use the transformer ratio
Vs / 120 = 200 / 1000
2
Find the turns ratio
200 / 1000 = 0.2
3
Solve for secondary voltage
Vs = 120 × 0.2 = 24 V

Equation 2: Power balance

Vp × Ip = Vs × Is
In an ideal transformer, input power equals output power.
1
Substitute the known output values
120 × Ip = 24 × 3
2
Compute the secondary power
24 × 3 = 72
3
Solve for primary current
Ip = 72 / 120 = 0.6 A
