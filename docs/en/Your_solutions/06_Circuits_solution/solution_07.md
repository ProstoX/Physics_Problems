## 7. Capacitors in Parallel

Two capacitors, $C_1=4\,\mu\text{F}$ and $C_2=6\,\mu\text{F}$, are connected in parallel to a 10 V battery. What is the total charge stored on the capacitors? What is the total energy stored?

---

### **Variable Definitions & Units**


* **$C_1, C_2$ (Capacitance):** The ability of a component to store an electric charge, measured in Farads ($\text{F}$). In this problem, we use microfarads ($\mu\text{F}$), where $1\,\mu\text{F} = 10^{-6}\,\text{F}$.
* **$V$ (Voltage / Potential Difference):** The electrical potential difference across the capacitors, measured in Volts ($\text{V}$).
* **$Q_{\text{total}}$ (Total Charge):** The total amount of electric charge stored in the system, measured in Coulombs ($\text{C}$).
* **$W$ or $U$ (Energy):** The electrical potential energy stored in the electric field of the capacitors, measured in Joules ($\text{J}$).

---

### **Step-by-Step Solution**

#### **1. Calculate Total Capacitance ($C_{\text{total}}$)**

When capacitors are connected in **parallel**, the total capacitance is the simple sum of the individual capacitances:


$$C_{\text{total}} = C_1 + C_2$$

$$C_{\text{total}} = 4\,\mu\text{F} + 6\,\mu\text{F} = 10\,\mu\text{F} = 10 \times 10^{-6}\,\text{F}$$

#### **2. Calculate Total Charge ($Q_{\text{total}}$)**

The charge stored on a capacitor is given by the formula $Q = C \cdot V$. For the total system:


$$Q_{\text{total}} = C_{\text{total}} \cdot V$$


Substituting our values ($V = 10\,\text{V}$):


$$Q_{\text{total}} = (10 \times 10^{-6}\,\text{F}) \cdot 10\,\text{V} = 100 \times 10^{-6}\,\text{C} = 100\,\mu\text{C}$$

#### **3. Calculate Total Energy Stored ($W$)**

The energy stored in a capacitor is given by $W = \frac{1}{2} C V^2$. Using the total capacitance:


$$W = \frac{1}{2} \cdot C_{\text{total}} \cdot V^2$$

$$W = \frac{1}{2} \cdot (10 \times 10^{-6}\,\text{F}) \cdot (10\,\text{V})^2$$

$$W = 0.5 \cdot (10 \times 10^{-6}) \cdot 100$$

$$W = 500 \times 10^{-6}\,\text{J} = 0.5 \times 10^{-3}\,\text{J} = 0.5\,\text{mJ}$$

**Final Answer:**

* The total charge stored is **$100\,\mu\text{C}$**.
* The total energy stored is **$0.5\,\text{mJ}$**.
