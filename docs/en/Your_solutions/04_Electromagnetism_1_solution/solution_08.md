## 8. Lorentz Force

A charged particle with charge $q = 2 \times 10^{-19}$ C and mass $m = 4 \times 10^{-27}$ kg enters a magnetic field of $B = 0.5$ T at a speed of $v = 10^6$ m/s perpendicular to the field. What is the magnitude of the Lorentz force acting on the particle?

---

### **Variables and Units**
Before calculating, here is what each letter in the relevant physics formula represents:
*   **$F$**: Lorentz force. The magnetic force exerted on the particle. Measured in **Newtons (N)**.
*   **$q$**: Electric charge. The amount of charge the particle carries. Measured in **Coulombs (C)**.
*   **$v$**: Velocity. The speed and direction of the particle. Measured in **meters per second (m/s)**.
*   **$B$**: Magnetic field. The strength of the magnetic field. Measured in **Teslas (T)**.
*   **$m$**: Mass of the particle. Measured in **kilograms (kg)**. *(Note: Mass is not needed to calculate the magnetic force, but it would be used if you needed to find the radius of the particle's circular path or its acceleration).*
*   **$\theta$**: Angle. The angle between the velocity vector and the magnetic field vector. Measured in **degrees (^\circ)** or radians.

---

### **Step-by-Step Solution**

**Step 1: Identify the correct formula.**
The magnitude of the magnetic Lorentz force on a moving charge is given by the equation:
$$F = |q|vB\sin(\theta)$$

**Step 2: Determine the angle ($\theta$).**
The problem states that the particle enters the magnetic field *perpendicularly*. This means the angle between the velocity vector and the magnetic field lines is 90 degrees.
Since $\sin(90^\circ) = 1$, the formula simplifies to:
$$F = |q|vB$$

**Step 3: Substitute the given values into the formula.**
*   $q = 2 \times 10^{-19}$ C
*   $v = 10^6$ m/s
*   $B = 0.5$ T

$$F = (2 \times 10^{-19}) \times (10^6) \times (0.5)$$

**Step 4: Calculate the final magnitude.**
Multiply the coefficients: $2 \times 0.5 = 1$
Multiply the powers of 10: $10^{-19} \times 10^6 = 10^{-13}$

$$F = 1 \times 10^{-13}\text{ N}$$

**Answer:** The magnitude of the Lorentz force acting on the particle is **$1 \times 10^{-13}$ N**.
