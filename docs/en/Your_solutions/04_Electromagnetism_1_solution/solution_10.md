## 10. Lorentz Force acting on Wire

A straight wire 2.0 m long carries a current of 10 A. It is placed in a uniform magnetic field of B = 0.5 T. Calculate the magnetic force on the wire if the angle between the wire and the magnetic field is:

a) $90^\circ$
b) $45^\circ$
c) $0^\circ$

---

### **Variables and Units**
Before we calculate, here is a quick breakdown of what each letter in the formula represents:
*   **$F$**: Magnetic Force. The force exerted on the wire by the magnetic field. Measured in **Newtons (N)**.
*   **$I$**: Current. The rate of flow of electric charge through the wire. Measured in **Amperes (A)**.
*   **$L$**: Length. The length of the wire that is inside the magnetic field. Measured in **meters (m)**.
*   **$B$**: Magnetic Field. The strength of the uniform magnetic field. Measured in **Teslas (T)**.
*   **$\theta$**: Angle. The angle between the direction of the current (the wire) and the direction of the magnetic field. Measured in **degrees (^\circ)** or radians.

---

### **Step-by-Step Solution**

**Step 1: Identify the correct formula.**
The magnitude of the magnetic force on a straight, current-carrying wire in a uniform magnetic field is given by the equation:
$$F = ILB\sin(\theta)$$

**Step 2: List the known given values.**
*   $I = 10$ A
*   $L = 2.0$ m
*   $B = 0.5$ T

We can simplify the constant part of the equation before plugging in the angles:
$$I \times L \times B = 10 \times 2.0 \times 0.5 = 10$$
So, the formula for this specific wire simplifies to:
$$F = 10 \sin(\theta)$$

**Step 3: Calculate for each angle.**

**a) When the angle is $90^\circ$ (perpendicular):**
$$F = 10 \sin(90^\circ)$$
Since $\sin(90^\circ) = 1$:
$$F = 10 \times 1 = 10\text{ N}$$
*Answer a: 10 N*

**b) When the angle is $45^\circ$:**
$$F = 10 \sin(45^\circ)$$
Since $\sin(45^\circ) = \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2} \approx 0.707$:
$$F = 10 \times 0.707 \approx 7.07\text{ N}$$
*Answer b: 7.07 N*

**c) When the angle is $0^\circ$ (parallel):**
$$F = 10 \sin(0^\circ)$$
Since $\sin(0^\circ) = 0$:
$$F = 10 \times 0 = 0\text{ N}$$
*Answer c: 0 N (No magnetic force acts on a wire parallel to the magnetic field).*
