## 5. Echo Ranging

A person shouts towards a cliff and hears the echo 1 seconds later. How far away is the cliff? (Speed of sound in air is 343 m/s).

---

### **Variables & Units**
Before solving, let's define the letters we will use in our formulas:
* **$d$**: Distance to the cliff. Measured in meters (**m**).
* **$v$**: Speed of sound. Measured in meters per second (**m/s**).
* **$t$**: Total time it takes to hear the echo. Measured in seconds (**s**).

---

### **Step-by-Step Solution**

**1. Understand the physics of an echo**
When a person shouts, the sound wave has to travel all the way to the cliff, bounce off it, and travel all the way back to the person's ears. This means the total distance the sound travels is twice the distance to the cliff ($2d$).

**2. State the known values**
* $v = 343$ m/s
* $t = 1$ s

**3. Choose the approach**
You can solve this in two ways: either halve the total time (to find the time it takes to reach the cliff just once) or halve the total distance at the end. We will halve the time, as it is often easier to conceptualize.
$$t_{one\_way} = \frac{t}{2}$$
$$t_{one\_way} = \frac{1}{2} = 0.5\text{ s}$$

**4. Apply the distance formula**
The fundamental physics formula for constant speed is distance equals speed multiplied by time:
$$d = v \cdot t_{one\_way}$$

**5. Calculate the final distance**
Multiply the speed of sound by the one-way time to find the distance between the person and the cliff.
$$d = 343 \cdot 0.5$$
$$d = 171.5\text{ m}$$

**Answer:** The cliff is **171.5 meters** away.
