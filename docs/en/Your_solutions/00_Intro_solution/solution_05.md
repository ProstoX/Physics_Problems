### Step 1: Identify the Given Information

First, we define the known values for the magnitude of the vector and its angle relative to the positive x-axis (the horizontal).

* Magnitude of vector $\vec{A}$: 15
* Angle $\theta$: 60°

### Step 2: Set Up the Equations

To break a vector down into its components, we use right-triangle trigonometry (sine and cosine).

* **Horizontal component ($A_x$):** This corresponds to the adjacent side of the triangle, so we use the cosine function.

$$A_x = |\vec{A}| \cos(\theta)$$


* **Vertical component ($A_y$):** This corresponds to the opposite side of the triangle, so we use the sine function.

$$A_y = |\vec{A}| \sin(\theta)$$



### Step 3: Calculate the Horizontal Component ($A_x$)

Substitute the known magnitude and angle into the horizontal equation:


$$A_x = 15 \cdot \cos(60^\circ)$$

Evaluate the trigonometric function (we know that $\cos(60^\circ) = 0.5$):


$$A_x = 15 \cdot 0.5$$

$$A_x = 7.5$$

### Step 4: Calculate the Vertical Component ($A_y$)

Substitute the known magnitude and angle into the vertical equation:


$$A_y = 15 \cdot \sin(60^\circ)$$

Evaluate the trigonometric function (we know that $\sin(60^\circ) = \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$):


$$A_y = 15 \cdot \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$$

$$A_y = 7.5\sqrt{3}$$

*(If you need a decimal approximation: $7.5 \cdot 0.866 \approx 12.99$)*

### Final Answer

* The horizontal component is **7.5**.
* The vertical component is **$7.5\sqrt{3}$** (or approximately **12.99**).
