Why 45° Maximises Range

Fixed initial velocity v₀ = 50 m/s · g = 9.8 m/s². Three simultaneous trajectories prove the theorem.

The 45° Theorem — Range is given by R = v₀² sin(2θ) / g. This is maximised when sin(2θ) = 1, i.e. 2θ = 90°, so θ = 45°. Complementary angles (30° & 60°) always share the same range since sin(60°) = sin(120°).
