## 10. Speed of Light in Media

What is the speed of light in a diamond, which has an index of refraction $n = 2.42$?

---

### Variables and Units

Before solving the problem, here is a quick breakdown of the symbols used in the refractive index formula and their standard units:

*   **$v$**: The speed of light in the specific material (medium). Measured in **meters per second (m/s)**.
*   **$c$**: The speed of light in a perfect vacuum. This is a universal physical constant, approximately equal to **$3.00 \times 10^8 \text{ m/s}$**.
*   **$n$**: The index of refraction of the material. It is a **dimensionless** quantity (it has no units) because it is simply a ratio of two speeds ($c$ divided by $v$). It describes how much light slows down when passing through the material.

---

### Step-by-Step Solution

**Given:**
*   Index of refraction for diamond ($n$) = $2.42$
*   Speed of light in a vacuum ($c$) $\approx 3.00 \times 10^8 \text{ m/s}$

**Step 1: State the formula for the index of refraction**
The index of refraction is defined as the ratio of the speed of light in a vacuum to the speed of light in the medium:
$$n = \frac{c}{v}$$

**Step 2: Rearrange the formula to solve for $v$**
Since we want to find the speed of light in the diamond ($v$), we multiply both sides by $v$ and divide both sides by $n$:
$$v = \frac{c}{n}$$

**Step 3: Substitute the known values into the equation**
$$v = \frac{3.00 \times 10^8 \text{ m/s}}{2.42}$$

**Step 4: Calculate the final value**
$$v \approx 1.23966 \times 10^8 \text{ m/s}$$

Rounding to three significant figures (matching the given value of 2.42):
$$v \approx 1.24 \times 10^8 \text{ m/s}$$

**Answer:**
The speed of light in a diamond is approximately **$1.24 \times 10^8 \text{ m/s}$**. (Notice that light travels at less than half its maximum speed when moving through a diamond due to its high optical density).
