## 7. Wavelength and Frequency

The human eye is most sensitive to light with a wavelength of about $550 \text{ nm}$. What color does this correspond to in the visible spectrum? What is the frequency of this light?

---

### Variables and Units

Before solving the problem, here is a quick overview of the symbols used and their standard SI units:

*   **$\lambda$ (lambda):** Wavelength. This is the physical distance between consecutive peaks of a wave. In standard SI units, it is measured in **meters (m)**, but for visible light, it is commonly expressed in **nanometers (nm)**, where $1 \text{ nm} = 10^{-9} \text{ m}$.
*   **$f$ (or $\nu$):** Frequency. This is the number of wave cycles that pass a fixed point per second. It is measured in **Hertz (Hz)**, which is equivalent to inverse seconds ($s^{-1}$).
*   **$c$:** The speed of light in a vacuum. This is a fundamental physical constant, approximately equal to **$3 \times 10^8 \text{ m/s}$**.

---

### Step-by-Step Solution

**Given:**
*   Wavelength $\lambda = 550 \text{ nm} = 550 \times 10^{-9} \text{ m}$
*   Speed of light $c \approx 3 \times 10^8 \text{ m/s}$

#### Step 1: Identify the Color
The visible light spectrum ranges roughly from $380 \text{ nm}$ (violet) to $750 \text{ nm}$ (red). 
*   A wavelength of exactly $550 \text{ nm}$ sits right in the middle of this spectrum.
*   **Answer:** $550 \text{ nm}$ corresponds to **yellow-green** (or a bright, vibrant green). This is precisely why high-visibility safety vests are usually this color; the human eye's photopic (daylight) vision peaks at this exact wavelength.

#### Step 2: Calculate the Frequency
The fundamental relationship between wave speed, wavelength, and frequency is given by the equation:
$$c = \lambda \cdot f$$

To find the frequency ($f$), we rearrange the formula:
$$f = \frac{c}{\lambda}$$

Substitute the given values (making sure to use meters for the wavelength to match the speed of light):
$$f = \frac{3 \times 10^8 \text{ m/s}}{550 \times 10^{-9} \text{ m}}$$

Calculate the division:
$$f \approx 0.005454 \times 10^{17} \text{ Hz}$$

Convert this to standard scientific notation:
$$f \approx 5.45 \times 10^{14} \text{ Hz}$$
*(Note: This is often expressed as $545 \text{ THz}$, or Terahertz).*

**Answer:** The frequency of this light is approximately **$5.45 \times 10^{14} \text{ Hz}$**.
